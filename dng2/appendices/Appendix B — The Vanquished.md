# Appendix B — The Vanquished

1.  **The Daemon (Luddenbrock Mines, Kaspien’s Laboratory)** — *Doctor Pepe* delivered the killing blow, driving his blade into the creature with surgical finality. The daemon had been restrained by *Scarlet’s* Entangle; struck three times with radiant energy by *Kragor’s* magic longsword; pierced by *Halite’s* trident; twice struck by *Elara’s* enchanted arrows; scorched by *Therin’s* burning hands and then seared by his Radiance of the Dawn; gashed by *Whisper’s* claws; and mauled twice by *Scarlet* in Moorbounder form. The killing was set up by *Kragor*, who—on death’s door—cast *Armor of Agathys* and deliberately offered himself as bait; the daemon bit down and the detonating ice armor blasted cold damage into its face and throat.

2.  **The Ruby Golem (Luddenbrock Mines, Kaspien’s Laboratory)** — *Scarlet* stopped the golem by seizing its command amulet while in rat form. The golem now answers to Scarlet.

3.  **Kaspien Vohlkrist, Lich (Luddenbrock Mines, Kaspien’s Laboratory)** — *Kragor* destroyed the lich, accidentally, by reaching for the fractured crystal vial with his spectral hand; the glass snapped in two, the soul-spark dissipated, and the eight-hundred-year-old body crumbled instantly to dust.

4.  **Flea, Homunculus (Luddenbrock Mines, Kaspien’s Laboratory)** — Ceased to exist the instant *Kragor* shattered Kaspien’s soul-vessel; with its master’s essence gone, its tether to the mortal plane snapped and the creature simply vanished.

5.  **Frost Giant, Greataxe (The Mountain Pass)** — *Hilda Brackmar* delivered the killing blow, opening the giant's throat with her battleaxe and driving a second stroke into its chest. The giant had been wounded by *Kragor's* Eldritch Blast and psychic daggers; pierced by crossbow bolts from *Doctor Pepe* and *Brennik*; slashed by the echoes of *Aedric* and *Vornesh*; struck by *Elara's* magic missiles; and mauled by *Hilda's* axe in the initial exchange.

6.  **Frost Giantess, Greatsword (The Mountain Pass)** — Executed by *Elara* with three streaks of force to the back of the head. The giantess had been forced to grovel by *Elara's* command; carved by the echoes of *Vornesh* and *Aedric*; hacked by *Hilda's* battleaxe; struck by crossbow bolts from *Doctor Pepe* and *Brennik*; wounded by *Kragor's* Eldritch Blast; weakened by *Therin's* necrotic tolling; scorched by *Serath's* holy radiance; and battered by his spectral mace.

7.  **Roper, the First (The Underdark, Faerzress caverns)** — *Therin* finished it with a searing ray, the stony hide sagging and cracking apart in the violet light. It had been battered down by *Elara's* repeated magic missiles; mauled by *Scarlet* in moorbounder form; savaged by her summoned puma, *Tiny*; and ground away by *Kragor's* teleported Cloud of Daggers.

8.  **Roper, the Second (The Underdark, Faerzress caverns)** — *Vornesh* killed it with a thrown spear that punched clean through the joint of its body. It had been struck by crossbow bolts from *Doctor Pepe* and *Brennik*; torn by *Scarlet* in moorbounder form; battered by *Serath's* spectral mace; and whittled down by *Kragor's* teleported Cloud of Daggers.

9.  **Roper, the Eldest (The Underdark, Faerzress caverns)** — *Therin* ended it with a mote of Sacred Flame, the last roper coming apart in radiant fire. The largest of the three, it had been opened three times over by *Vornesh's* poisoned scimitars; struck by bolts from *Brennik* and *Doctor Pepe*; pierced by *Elara's* arrows; cursed by *Serath's* Bane and lit by *Therin's* Faerie Fire; and ground down by *Kragor's* drifting daggers as it grappled the party in its dying spasm.

10. **The Oozes (The Underdark)** — *Therin* delivered the final strike, incinerating the last of the split jellies with a burst of searing fire. Five amber-grey adhesive jellies ambushed the party from small wall openings in a low five-to-ten-foot tunnel. The creatures burned through flesh with acid, knocking *Doctor Pepe* unconscious twice—both times restored by *Scarlet’s* rapid healing magic—and split into smaller hungry masses when slashed by *Vornesh* and her echo’s scimitars. The creatures were battered, boiled, and dissolved by *Therin’s* mace, holy radiance, and searing flame; *Kragor’s* cold and necrotic eldritch blasts; *Brennik’s* warpick; *Vornesh’s* spear, blades, and echo; *Elara’s* ice magic and arrows; *Scarlet’s* shifting column of moonlight; and *Doctor Pepe’s* relentless dagger strikes.

11. **Derro at the Barrier (The Underdark)** — No one saw these die. Running from the war camp with wings gaining behind them, the company sealed a narrow place twice over: _Kragor’s_ Cloud of Daggers turning in a few feet of air, and _Scarlet’s_ column of cold moonlight dropped in behind it, filling the gap wall to wall. The pursuit stopped at the barrier and argued about it in Abyssal. Three or four derro came on anyway, whether sent or of their own choosing, and the company heard them reach the blades and the light, and heard them stop.

12. **Derro Explorer, the Groveler (The Underdark)** — _Kragor_ killed it with a blast of eldritch force, after his mind had failed to shove it aside. It had been seared by _Therin’s_ Scorching Ray; twice torn by _Kragor’s_ Screaming Maelstrom, which it knelt inside; shot by _Brennik’s_ crossbow; chilled by the burst of _Elara’s_ Ice Knife; torn by _Scarlet’s_ Spike Growth; and forced to its knees by _Therin’s_ Command.

13. **Derro Explorer, the Climber (The Underdark)** — _Doctor Pepe_ stabbed it dead beside the lantern he had just lit and set down. It had been struck by the shard and burst of _Elara’s_ Ice Knife and torn by _Scarlet’s_ Spike Growth before climbing the wall to reach the rogue, where _Brennik’s_ warpick and two strokes of _Vornesh’s_ scimitars found it.

14. **Derro Guards, the Crossbowmen (The Underdark)** — _Elara_ killed both with a single Ice Knife, the shard striking one and the cold burst taking the pair. They had climbed out of _Scarlet’s_ Spike Growth to shoot at Scarlet in dire wolf form; one bolt went wide, and the other, which would have struck, was spoiled by _Elara’s_ Silvery Barbs. One of the two had already been badly burned by _Therin’s_ Scorching Ray.

15. **Derro Guard, at the Echo (The Underdark)** — _Kragor_ killed it by moving his Screaming Maelstrom onto it. It had been seared by _Therin’s_ Scorching Ray, chilled by _Elara’s_ Ice Knife, and torn by _Scarlet’s_ Spike Growth before it climbed the wall to strike _Vornesh’s_ echo.

16. **Derro Guard, the Last Guard (The Underdark)** — _Elara_ killed it with an arrow from her shortbow, through the eye. It had been torn by _Scarlet’s_ Spike Growth; tolled by _Therin’s_ Toll of the Dead; and pierced by _Vornesh’s_ thrown spear; it had missed Vornesh once and struck her echo.

17. **Derro Explorer, the Beheaded (The Underdark)** — _Vornesh_ took its head off with a single stroke of her scimitar. It had been torn by _Scarlet’s_ Spike Growth; jumped to clear _Doctor Pepe’s_ ball bearings and fallen anyway; been shoved back into them by _Kragor’s_ telekinesis; and had _Kragor’s_ Screaming Maelstrom moved onto it twice. It swung at _Brennik_ and at _Scarlet_ in dire wolf form, and missed both.

18. **Derro Explorer, the Last (The Underdark)** — _Elara_ finished it mid-word with darts of force from her rod, and with it the hunting party. It had been torn by _Scarlet’s_ Spike Growth; struck by _Brennik’s_ warpick; shot by _Doctor Pepe’s_ crossbow; bitten down by _Scarlet_ in dire wolf form; and had shrugged off _Therin’s_ Toll of the Dead and stabbed Scarlet before _Doctor Pepe_ ran and leapt onto it, knife first, and the wolf nipped it once more.
